Shetkari Mitra (शेतकरी मित्र) is an agricultural decision-support engine developed by the Viksit Minds engineering team. Built exclusively for Maharashtra's vegetable growing corridors, it transforms volatile wholesale auction records from 20 primary APMC yards into reliable 5, 7, and 14-day price projections. By pairing gradient-boosted time-series intelligence with crop perishability risks, it delivers clear SELL NOW vs. WAIT guidance to prevent distress sales.

Why Simple Price Prediction Isn't Enough

A projected price rise of 4% sounds positive, but holding perishable tomatoes for 7 days typically causes 8% to 10% weight loss and spoilage. Shetkari Mitra incorporates real post-harvest science: crops only receive a 'WAIT' recommendation when the forecast comfortably exceeds their spoilage decay threshold.
